Kiosk audio check

Run this on the kiosk, in the same Chrome, with the speakers and mic Mia uses. A beep plays the whole time; the mic opens and closes every 10 seconds. The beep must stay exactly as loud when the mic is on. If it drops in MIC ON, the kiosk is lowering playback while the mic is open — see the checklist.

All on = what Mia's page asks for (livekit-client defaults). Change them only between runs.
